ABOUT NOIR STORE

Good chocolate doesn't need to be complicated.

Established with a love for authentic cocoa, NOIR started as a boutique confectionery stall in Lucena City. Our vision is simple: to make world-class European and Swiss chocolates accessible locally without the heavy retail markup found in big shopping centers.

Store Profile & Heritage

Founded in 2024 by Jose Pundan, NOIR has served thousands of sweet lovers across Quezon Province with directly imported items, fresh artisan brews, and curated candy gift boxes.
